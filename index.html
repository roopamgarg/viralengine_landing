How it works

Integrate first. Automate what slows you down.

We don't ask agencies to rip out their stack. Agents work across Slack, Google Workspace, Notion, Airtable, CRMs, and reporting systems — with humans deciding what ships.

01

Discover bottlenecks

We map the operational work that doesn't create client value — coordination, reporting, follow-ups, documentation — and prioritize the highest-leverage workflows.

02

Deploy & integrate

Launch pre-built agents or custom ones designed for your process. Connect them to the tools your team already lives in.

03

Keep humans in the loop

Agents recommend, organize, and automate routine tasks. Important business decisions stay with account managers, campaign leads, and founders.

04

Measure outcomes

Success is hours of manual work eliminated, faster campaign turnaround, and happier clients — not AI usage for its own sake.
