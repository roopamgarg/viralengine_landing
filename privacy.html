Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

ViralEngine (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides a platform that lets businesses connect their WhatsApp Business account and manage WhatsApp communication through our app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to visitors of our website, users of the ViralEngine platform, and anyone whose information is processed when a customer connects a WhatsApp Business account and handles messaging through our services.

If you use ViralEngine on behalf of a business, that business is typically the data controller for message content and end-customer contacts. ViralEngine processes that data as a service provider to deliver the platform.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile information

When you create or manage an account, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and contact details
  • Business name, role, and workspace settings
  • Login credentials and authentication data
  • Billing and subscription information, where applicable

WhatsApp Business connection data

When you connect a WhatsApp Business account (including via Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform APIs), we may collect and process:

  • WhatsApp Business Account identifiers and phone numbers linked to the account
  • Business profile details made available through the connection (for example display name, about text, and profile picture)
  • Authorization tokens and connection status needed to keep the account linked
  • Webhook and delivery metadata required to send and receive messages

Messaging and communication data

To let you handle WhatsApp communication in our app, we process message-related data associated with your connected account, which may include:

  • Inbound and outbound message content (text, media, templates, interactive messages)
  • Sender and recipient phone numbers and WhatsApp identifiers
  • Timestamps, delivery and read status, conversation threads, and related metadata
  • Labels, tags, notes, assignments, and other organizational data you add in the app
  • Contact and customer profile information you import or sync for messaging workflows

Usage, device, and technical data

  • IP address, browser type, device information, and approximate location derived from IP
  • Product usage events (for example feature interactions, session activity, and performance logs)
  • Cookies or similar technologies used for authentication, security, and site functionality

Support and correspondence

If you contact us (for example at hello@viralengine.ai), we keep the content of that communication and related metadata so we can respond and improve support.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the platform, including WhatsApp account connection and messaging
  • Authenticate users, secure accounts, and prevent abuse or unauthorized access
  • Send, receive, store, display, search, and organize WhatsApp conversations on your behalf
  • Deliver product features such as templates, routing, automation, analytics, and notifications you enable
  • Process payments, manage subscriptions, and communicate service-related notices
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests
  • Monitor reliability, debug issues, and improve product performance
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use WhatsApp message content to build advertising profiles for third parties.

4. WhatsApp, Meta, and third-party services

WhatsApp messaging is provided through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. When you connect your WhatsApp Business account, Meta’s terms and privacy practices also apply to data processed by Meta / WhatsApp.

You are responsible for using the integration in line with WhatsApp Business and Meta policies, including rules on opt-in consent, customer care windows, template messaging, and prohibited content.

We may also use infrastructure and service providers (for example hosting, databases, analytics, email, and payment processors) that process data only as needed to run ViralEngine and under appropriate contractual safeguards.

5. How we share information

We may share information in these limited situations:

  • Service providers who help us host, operate, secure, or support the platform
  • Meta / WhatsApp as required to connect accounts and transmit messages
  • Your organization, including admins and teammates you invite to a workspace
  • Legal and safety disclosures when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, security, and integrity
  • Business transfers in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate protections

6. Data retention

We retain account, connection, and messaging data for as long as needed to provide the service, meet contractual commitments, resolve disputes, enforce policies, and comply with legal requirements.

Message history and related conversation data are generally retained while your account remains active and for a reasonable period afterward, unless you delete data earlier through available product controls or request deletion where applicable. Retention periods may vary by feature, legal obligation, and backup systems.

7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including access controls, encryption in transit where appropriate, and monitoring of our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for safeguarding your login credentials and for managing access permissions within your workspace and connected WhatsApp Business account.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@viralengine.ai. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. If you are an end customer messaging a business that uses ViralEngine, please contact that business directly for requests about their WhatsApp conversations; we will assist our customers as required.

You can disconnect a WhatsApp Business account from ViralEngine through the product settings (or by revoking access in Meta). Disconnecting stops future syncing through our app; previously stored data may remain until deleted under our retention practices or a valid deletion request.

9. International transfers

We may process and store information in countries other than where you are located. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

10. Children’s privacy

ViralEngine is built for business use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, when appropriate, provide additional notice through the website or product. Continued use of ViralEngine after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
